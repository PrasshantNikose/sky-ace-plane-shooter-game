# sky-ace-plane-shooter-game
Sky Ace is a free plane shooting game you can play right in your browser. There's nothing to download and no sign-up. Climb into the green propeller plane, shoot down the enemy squadron and see how long you can stay in the air.
[Put your game here]
Click the game once, then press Play. Use the full-screen button in the corner for the best view.
How to Play
Move: W, A, S, D or the arrow keys
Shoot: Space bar
Restart after a crash: Enter
On a phone or tablet: drag your finger to fly and hold the FIRE button to shoot
Game Rules
You start with 3 lives. Enemy bullets and crashing into a plane each cost you one life. Small enemy planes go down in one hit. The big purple planes take four hits and shoot back, so keep moving. The longer you survive, the faster the enemies come.
Tips for a Higher Score
Stay in the left half of the screen so you have time to react.
Shoot in short bursts and keep moving up and down.
Take out the big purple planes first, because they fire at you.
Don't fly too low, since falling planes can still hit you.
Questions and Answers

Yes, it's completely free to play online.
