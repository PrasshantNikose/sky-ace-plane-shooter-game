How to fly

W A S D or ← ↑ ↓ → move

Rules

  • You have 3 lives. Enemy bullets and collisions both cost one.
  • Small planes die in one hit. Big purple planes need four and shoot back.
  • Enemies get faster and more frequent the longer you last.

The squadron

Sky Ace is a browser game. Plays best with a keyboard or a touch screen.
